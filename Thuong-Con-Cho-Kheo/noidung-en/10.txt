## Your child's heart is broken
BC: For three days Khang, in grade 11, has skipped meals and lain face down on his pillow, eyes swollen. His little sister reports: Ngan dumped him. Dad walks in and sees his seventeen-year-old, taller than himself, curled up like a small boy.

CON: (Muffled.) I'm fine.
BA: Right. When I was twenty someone left me, and I said I was fine too. Then I rode around the city until two in the morning. It really hurt; it wasn't pretend.
CON: (A long silence.) She said I'm childish. She's with a grade 12 guy now.
BA: Hearing that must feel like a punch in the chest.
CON: Whatever I do, I see her. I open my phone and there are photos of her with him.

VS: To adults a school romance is a small thing. To a child it is the first great loss of their life, and the adolescent brain processes social rejection almost as strongly as physical pain.
